# Crow Simulator

You are a crow on a mission. That mission is to fly around and collect giant blueberries that grew around the area. You can only grab one blueberry at a time and you have to put the blueberries in your basket to score a point. To move you must use WASD and you automatically pick up a blueberry if you over one.
